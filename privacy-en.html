Privacy Policy

Noctiluca · Glitter Tech Solutions LTD · Effective 6 October 2026

РусскийEnglish

Noctiluca sends almost nothing. The game has no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising networks. Its one network request checks the time of a new personal best with our server; the device advertising identifier goes with that request only if you allow tracking.

Information that is sent

When you set a new personal best, the game asks our server for the current time (api.platform-core.com) so the record's time can be shown as checked. Before the first such request, iOS asks whether you allow tracking:

  • If you allow it, the request also carries the device advertising identifier (IDFA). We may use it for advertising. The identifier is not linked to your name, an account, or any other data in the game.
  • If you decline, the game never reads or sends the advertising identifier. The record keeps the device time, and the game works in full.

Like any network request, contacting our server reveals your device's IP address to it. Your score, camera frames, sound, photos, and coordinates are not part of the request and never leave your device. Apart from that request, we receive and keep no data about you.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally, in the app's private storage:

  • Your best score with the date it was set and whether that time was checked with our server, the greatest depth reached, and the number of completed dives.
  • Your chosen glow colour and water backdrop colours, stored as colour values only.
  • Game settings: sound and haptics.
  • Which tutorial hints you have already seen.

This data is never uploaded. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

Device permissions

Every permission is optional, is requested only when you use the feature that needs it, and declining it leaves the rest of the game fully playable.

  • Camera — to read the colour in front of the lens and turn it into your creature's glow. The video is analysed live in memory for colour only. No photo or video is captured, saved, or transmitted.
  • Microphone — to measure how loud the room is, which calibrates the sonar ring in the Field Station. Audio is analysed in real time for volume only. Nothing is recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Photo library (read) — to let you pick one photo whose two dominant colours become your water backdrop. Only the colours are used. The photo itself is not copied, saved, or transmitted.
  • Photo library (add) — to save the specimen card image to your library when you ask the game to save it.
  • Location (while using the app) — read once, at kilometre accuracy, and immediately reduced to a single number that seeds the currents of your next dive. Your position is rounded to roughly a 10 km cell, is never written to storage, and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time in the Field Station.
  • Notifications — local reminders only, scheduled on your device. We do not operate a push service and cannot send you messages.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — asked once, before the first time check of a record. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Advertising identifier and tracking

Noctiluca shows no ads and collects no analytics. The device advertising identifier is sent to our server only with your permission, as described above, and we may use it for advertising. The game does not store the identifier or combine it with any other information. We share nothing with data brokers. Declining tracking, or withdrawing it in iOS Settings, does not affect the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information about children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, and no analytics services; it uses only Apple's own system frameworks. The only server the game contacts is our own, api.platform-core.com: it receives the time check request and, with your permission, the advertising identifier. We do not pass that data on to third parties.

Your rights

Apart from the advertising identifier received with your consent, which is not linked to your identity, we hold no data about you. Everything the game keeps lives on your device and is under your control: you can reset it in the game's settings or by deleting the app. You can stop the advertising identifier from being sent in iOS Settings, and reset the identifier there too.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
