Privacy Policy

Noctiluca · Glitter Tech Solutions LTD · Effective 19 August 2026

Noctiluca does not collect any data. The game has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising networks, and no servers operated by us. The app contains no networking code at all: it cannot send anything anywhere, even in principle.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. No data generated by the game leaves your device, and we have no ability to access it.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally, in the app's private storage:

  • Your best score, greatest depth reached, and number of completed dives.
  • Your chosen glow colour and water backdrop colours, stored as colour values only.
  • Game settings: interface language, sound, haptics, and background audio.
  • Which tutorial hints you have already seen.

This data is never uploaded. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

Device permissions

Every permission is optional, is requested only when you open the feature that needs it, and declining it leaves the rest of the game fully playable.

  • Camera — to read the colour in front of the lens and turn it into your creature's glow. The video is analysed live in memory for colour only. No photo or video is captured, saved, or transmitted.
  • Microphone — to measure how loud the room is, which calibrates the sonar ring in the Field Station. Audio is analysed in real time for volume only. Nothing is recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Photo library (read) — to let you pick one photo whose two dominant colours become your water backdrop. Only the colours are used. The photo itself is not copied, saved, or transmitted.
  • Photo library (add) — to save the specimen card image to your library when you ask the game to save it.
  • Location (while using the app) — read once, at kilometre accuracy, and immediately reduced to a single number that seeds the currents of your next dive. Your position is rounded to roughly a 10 km cell, is never written to storage, and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time in the Field Station.
  • Notifications — local reminders only, scheduled on your device. We do not operate a push service and cannot send you messages.

Advertising identifier and tracking

Noctiluca shows no ads and collects no analytics. The Field Station includes a tracking permission panel that exists so the permission is genuinely implemented and inspectable. If you allow tracking, the game reads your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and displays part of it on screen so you can see what the permission controls. The identifier is never stored, never transmitted, and never combined with any other information. We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and we do not share any data with data brokers. If you decline, nothing about the game changes.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, and no analytics services. It uses only Apple's own system frameworks and communicates with no servers whatsoever.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete on request. All data lives on your device and is under your control: you can reset it in the game's settings or by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
